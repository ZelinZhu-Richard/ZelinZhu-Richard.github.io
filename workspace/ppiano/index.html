A QUICK FIELD GUIDE

There’s music
in your hands.

Gesture Synth

Face the camera with palms visible. Raise 1–5 left fingers for chords I–V. Index + pinky selects VI; add your thumb for VII. Tilt inward for major, outward for minor.

On your right hand, 1–4 fingers select the root voicing, first inversion, seventh, and dominant/diminished seventh. Extend your thumb for a lower octave. Raise your hand for volume and tilt it to shape the tone.

Try Left only for simpler playing. If the labels feel reversed, use Swap left/right roles.

Paper Piano EXPERIMENTAL

Draw the layout below on landscape paper with a dark marker. Keep the outer border straight and fill the five black keys. Put C at the left; black keys must appear in groups of two and three.

Fix the camera at an angle, not directly overhead. Scan with your hands out of the way. Confirm the detected keys, then calibrate the fingers you want to use. Lift each finger before tapping. Recalibrate whenever the paper or camera moves.

Contact is estimated from one camera. Real-device accuracy targets are not yet measured; this is an experimental instrument.
