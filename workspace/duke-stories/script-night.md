# Story B: The Night Duke Lost Its Blue

Opening label: **A fictional campus adventure**

**1. Midnight — blue lifts from campus as luminous sparks; the mascot’s color fades.**

> At midnight, Duke's blue slipped out of the world.

> The Blue Devil noticed immediately.

> “This is going to be a problem.”

**2. An unexpected assignment — the Blue Devil meets black-dragon Richard and hands him a lantern.**

> Richard had only come to look around.

> Now the Blue Devil was handing him a very empty lantern.

> “You have wings, right?”

**3. Brodhead — a mischievous spark hides among curling noodles.**

> The first spark was hiding in Brodhead Center.

> In a bowl of noodles.

> It did not want to leave.

Scrolling carries the spark into the lantern; no clicking is required to advance.

**4. Across the quad — the Blue Devil gives chase; Richard flies over the winding path.**

> The second spark ran across the quad.

> The Blue Devil ran after it.

> Richard took the shortcut.

**5. Above the Chapel — the camera climbs the exterior toward the final spark.**

> The last spark was above the Chapel.

> The Blue Devil looked up.

> Richard opened his wings.

**6. The catch — the signature long sticky sequence: ascent, wind, a slipping lantern, and a coordinated rescue.**

> The wind took the lantern.

> The Blue Devil caught the cord.

> Richard caught the light.

Scrolling drives the ascent and catch. The composition briefly holds at maximum tension while native scrolling continues.

**7. Blue returns — a wave of color travels down the Chapel and across campus; Richard transforms.**

> Blue ran down the stone and back across the quad.

> Some of it stayed with Richard.

> New horns. Blue scales. The same dragon.

**8. Breakfast — warm dawn at Brodhead; the two sit together, with one tiny spark still watching from a bowl.**

> By sunrise, everything was almost normal.

> “Breakfast?” asked the Blue Devil.

> Richard checked the noodles for sparks.
