# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Users
Portfolio visitors experiencing Richard's imagined Duke future and a fictional campus adventure.

## Product Purpose
Two original illustrated scroll stories about building for people, belonging, growth and teamwork. Richard remains recognizably a dragon while acquiring Duke-blue scales and devil horns.

## Capabilities and Constraints
Two locked scripts, 45 narrative beats, three unlisted routes under /duke/. Original generated art, static HTML/CSS/JS, native reversible scroll effects, accessible static reading, no backend/audio/forms/added analytics. Direct links work; portfolio navigation and sitemap entries are absent, with build-generated noindex metadata. Committing, branch push and PR are authorized; merging and live deployment are not.

## Brand Commitments
Richard approved the four Duke character images with “Yes Approve”. Preserve the blue-and-white humanoid mascot and the dragon's face, purple eyes, galaxy wings, tail and pendant through transformation. Duke Royal Blue #00539B, Navy #012169, loose ink and flat comic shading, white Merriweather, black pauses, warm dawn endings. Code-led implementation extends the accepted Wings visual language; no new layout concept exercise.

## Evidence on Hand
Both exact scripts, approved art/gate-v1/, recorded prompts and public Duke references. Existing EqualVoice project route is /work/#equalvoice. Story A is imagined; story B is fiction.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Keyboard focus and replay, fixed motion toggle, complete reduced-motion/no-JS reading, contextual alternatives and separate mobile copy/art regions.
