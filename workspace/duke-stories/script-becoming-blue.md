# Story A: Becoming Blue

Opening label: **An imagined Duke future**

**1. At the edge — Richard arrives beneath stone arches; the Blue Devil approaches.**

> The Blue Devil found Richard at the edge of the quad.

> In his bag: a few working ideas. A few broken ones.

> “What are you making?”

**2. A reason to build — Richard opens a prototype showing uneven speech waveforms.**

> Richard unfolded a question he had brought from home.

> Why were some voices easier for a machine to hear?

> And what would it take to listen better?

**3. A larger table — Brodhead Center; the Blue Devil makes space as students join.**

> At Brodhead, one idea became a conversation.

> The table grew.

**4. Making together — a long sticky workshop sequence; components, tests, and contributions assemble with scrolling.**

> They tested. Listened. Tried again.

> Richard could reach places the others couldn't.

The Blue Devil organizes the moving pieces; Richard uses his wings to connect suspended components; students contribute revisions. The prototype’s separate voices become equally visible without implying that a real research problem has already been solved.

**5. A little blue — a quiet walk near the Chapel; the transformation begins.**

> Under the Chapel's shadow, Richard noticed the blue.

> First a scale. Then a wing.

> His pendant still held the same light.

**6. An open seat — return to the Brodhead table; another newcomer arrives.**

> The next newcomer found an open seat.

> Richard pulled it closer.

> There was room for another voice.

**7. Becoming — scrolling reveals Richard’s completed dragon–Blue Devil form.**

> By morning, his horns curved differently.

> His wings were still his.

**8. Final line and dawn credits — Richard, the Blue Devil, and their collaborators overlook the waking campus.**

> **I want to build things that help people belong.**
