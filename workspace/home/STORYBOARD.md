# Storyboard: The Instrument

One continuous scroll. It ends on the finished instrument floating in space with its seven spheres, which are the seven stones. Scrolling is the crank: every gear position is a function of scroll.

The look is ART.md's night and ink, engraved: hatching, clean ink outlines, one orange accent per frame. New for this concept: a star field behind everything in the space frames (small ink points of three sizes, sparse, a faint denser band; no colour, no glow, no nebula clouds).

## Who this is about

Richard's interests: management science and engineering (decisions under uncertainty), statistics, machine learning, startups, AI evaluation, logic and judgement. NOT neuroscience, biology, chemistry or physics: no neurons, brains, molecules or lab glassware anywhere. The instrument is a machine for forecasting and judging, not a science exhibit.

## The four ideas it combines

- **Dimensions grow** (point, line, plane, solid, motion): the story starts as one point, becomes a drawn line, a flat engraved plate, then a solid object, then a moving system. Growth is not along one axis; it adds axes.
- **Zero to one**: the instrument is built part by part as the story is told. Nothing is on screen that has not been earned.
- **Each object stands for one thing**: on the desk, four objects carry the four "what am I working on" chapters, and each hands one part to the instrument.
- **Suspense, twice**: the viewer is inside the mechanism before knowing what it is; then what looked like deep space turns out to be a desk at 3 a.m.; then the desk falls away and it really is space.
