# Gym surface brief
Mode: Operate, with an Experience game canvas. Follows the site's paper-and-ink rules in site/DESIGN.md. Gym does not load public/portfolio.css, so style.css starts with a copy of its tokens (including the `--sharks-*` block and the Neoris font face). Keep that copy in sync.
Page: paper (#F4F3EE) with ink (#161514) text, 1px solid borders, square corners, hard 4px ink shadow on button hover only. Headings in Neoris, reading text in the serif stack, labels, buttons and numbers in mono. Left-aligned; the intro is a 7/5 split and the workspace is a wide stage beside a 300px setup column.
Accent: burnt orange (#B93C0B) marks one number per screen: the rep count in Push-up Counter, the gaps-passed score in Flying Bird. Focus rings also use it. Do not use it for fills or other states.
Stage: the camera feed and game canvas sit on an ink stage. Overlays on it (status chip, rep count, score, countdown, game cover card) are solid paper or ink boxes with a 1px border. No translucency, blur, glow, gradients or text shadows. The game draws grey columns with paper rules at the gap edges, and a paper bird with an orange beak. No star field or decorative icons.
Copy is plain and factual. Keep the "Camera counting · experimental" label visible until physical accuracy is measured (see VALIDATION.md). Progress is stored only in this browser.
Prioritize large readable rep feedback, visible tracking state, keyboard access, and controls below the stage on phones. No camera permission on load. Game animation is essential; decorative motion and transitions stop under reduced motion.
