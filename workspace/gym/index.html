Push-up counter and Flying Bird

Counts push-ups from a side-on webcam view. One rep is up, down, up. Flying Bird uses the same body height to steer a bird through gaps. Tracking is experimental and its accuracy has not been measured.

Camera counting · experimental

Setup and rules

  1. Place a stationary camera to your side. Fit your shoulders, hands, hips, and feet in the frame. Face sideways, with your body across the view.
  2. Start the camera, then capture your raised and lowered positions. Each capture gives you five seconds to get into position.
  3. Return to the raised position. A full down-and-up movement counts one rep. Keep the same camera and body position.
  4. In Flying Bird, your body height sets the bird’s height: raised is high, lowered is low. Fly through the gaps. Space or Pause flight stops the game.

At least one completed push-up earns a streak day. Streaks use your device’s local date and stay in this browser. Demo play never adds workout reps.

Counting is an estimate, not a form assessment. Physical accuracy is not yet measured. Use a comfortable range of motion and stop if anything hurts.
