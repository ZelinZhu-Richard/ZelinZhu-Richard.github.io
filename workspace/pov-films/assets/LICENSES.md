# Asset licenses and credits

Every external file used by the POV films lives under `assets/`. Each download sits in its own folder.
Paths below are relative to `workspace/pov-films/`. Sizes are bytes as stored (MiB in brackets).
Pixel sizes were read from the files. Previews (max 1600 px) are in `assets/_previews/`, and
crop overlays for the recommended framings are in `assets/_previews/crops/` (see `SKY_GUIDE.md`).
