A little help opening your domain

Show your palms and look for the violet finger outlines. Gold dots mark your thumbs and index fingers. Spread those four fingertips into a window; your fingers can stay relaxed. Turn one hand upside down for a wider frame.

If only one outline appears, bring the other hand fully into view. Bright, even lighting helps. Crossed or overlapping hands can hide fingertips; separate them to restore your domain. No outlines? Move your hands closer to the camera, or Stop and start again.

Camera images are processed in this browser. Nothing is recorded or uploaded, and no microphone is requested. Switching tabs turns the camera off; press Start camera to resume.
