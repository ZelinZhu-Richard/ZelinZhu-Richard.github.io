How to release 尘遁

Face both open palms toward the camera. Bring your left and right thumb tips close together, and do the same with your index fingertips. Leave a window between the thumb pair and the index pair. A small gap is fine; touching is not required.

Hold the seal briefly. A white cube charges between your fingers and flies toward you, then breaks into dust. Separate your hands, then form the seal again to repeat. The gap tolerance scales with your hand size on screen; no exact distance in centimetres is measured.

Keep both palms at a similar distance from the camera and all four fingertips visible. If the seal is not recognised, follow the prompt along the bottom of the camera view. With reduced motion on, the cube stays still and fades, with no flight or particles.

Everything runs in this browser. Nothing is recorded or uploaded, and there is no microphone or sound. Switching effects keeps the same camera; Stop or leaving the tab turns it off.

How to cast a fireball

Show one open palm toward the camera and keep it visible for a moment. A fireball launches from your palm toward you. Close your hand briefly, then reopen it to cast again. Each hand casts on its own.

Keep your fingers visible and use even lighting. There is a short cooldown between casts. With reduced motion on, the fireball becomes a still glow that fades, with no flying embers.

Camera images stay in this browser. Nothing is recorded or uploaded, and no microphone is requested. Switching tabs turns the camera off; press Start camera to resume. Tracking uses the same local MediaPipe model as Domain Expansion.

How to frame the window

Show your palms and look for the violet finger outlines. Gold dots mark your thumbs and index fingers. Spread those four fingertips apart to make a window; your fingers can stay relaxed. Turn one hand upside down for a wider frame.

If only one outline appears, bring the other hand fully into view. Bright, even lighting helps. Crossed or overlapping hands can hide fingertips; separate them and the window comes back. No outlines? Move your hands closer to the camera, or Stop and start again.

Camera images are processed in this browser. Nothing is recorded or uploaded, and no microphone is requested. Switching tabs turns the camera off; press Start camera to resume.
