How it works

  1. 01Finish tasksPet food comes only from tasks. Harder tiers pay far more.
  2. 02Feed active pets100 a day keeps one alive and paying dividends. Extra = XP + egg meter.
  3. 03Eggs arriveA full meter lays an egg of the pet's tier. The streak pays more every night.
  4. 04Open: 3–12 itemsCoins, egg food, XP treats, diamonds, boosters. Sometimes one pet egg.
  5. 05Hatch95% its own tier, 5% one higher. Better food nudges the odds up.
  6. 06Merge or gambleTwins merge for a star. Different species can combine for a shot at the next tier.
  7. 07Save or spendBad luck? Diamonds buy an Epic egg in about half a year.

Stars & levels

  1. ★★2 copies · Twin Spark: egg meter +10%, XP +10%
  2. ★★★4 copies · Second Helping: +2 items in every egg it lays
  3. ★★★★8 copies · Golden Touch: dividends +25%, shards +25%, +1 item
  4. ★★★★★16 copies · Nine Lives: survives one death, once per lineage

Levels cap by tier: Ordinary 20 · Rare 30 · Super Rare 40 · Epic 55 · Mythic 70 · Legendary 85 · Ultra 100. Each level: +0.1% egg-meter speed and +1% dividends. Every 10 levels: +1 item in the eggs it lays. Skills unlock at 10, 25, 40, 55, 70, 85 and 100.

Trinkets

5 slots on Epic pets and up. Epic, Mythic and Legendary trinkets drop from Epic+ eggs or cost diamonds. Ultra trinkets are wildly overpowered: 10% from Ultra eggs, or a 24-hour flash sale after a big win, never below 15,000 diamonds even discounted.
